Quiet Tab

Before you rely on this This changes what your screen looks like to someone standing behind you. It does not change what your employer can see. If your work computer or network is monitored, your IT team can still see which sites you visit, and browser extensions may be visible to them too. Check your workplace's acceptable-use policy, and consider job hunting on your own device.
